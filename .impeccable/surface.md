## Public surface: Persuade

FIRST VIEWPORT: white company header, cream/navy fiberglass background, concise Vietnamese headline and green consultation action. A large real customer product photograph occupies the right. Mobile stacks copy and photograph. The generated background is decorative; all product evidence comes from the customer ZIPs.

After the hero: application-led catalogue, featured biogas/septic specifications, a plain-language consultation process, published knowledge/project content, FAQ, inquiry form and company footer. Empty news/project collections are handled honestly without invented installations.

## Administration: Operate

Stable sidebar and compact header, clear task labels. Primary workflow: write, save draft, preview, publish. Editor toolbar groups text, structure and media. SEO settings sit in an expandable panel with useful defaults. A media picker shows real uploaded images/video. Save state, validation and recovery are explicit.

## Layout and distill

Group controls by task before adding containers. Separate catalogue browsing from reading articles. Use rhythm between dense product rows and generous feature sections. Keep one main consultation action in each decision region. Advanced SEO and technical settings remain accessible but collapsed. Do not remove necessary publishing, media or editing capabilities in the name of simplicity.

## Craft constraints

Preserve Thanh Loi navy/green/white, Be Vietnam Pro Vietnamese typography, strong contrast and natural wrapping. ICS workbench forbids artificial line balancing, NBSP binding and faded gray text. Use semantic structure, visible focus, reduced motion and responsive layouts from 360px. Rendering and independent review are required before delivery.

## Product studio art direction and motion

Latest owner goal: more composed frames, expressive typography, patterned background and visible animation, with the detail of a designed campaign rather than a plain landing page. Taste-skill cinematic-product references guide the photographic composition; Impeccable animate guides timing and accessibility. The existing brand colors, factual copy and real customer photographs remain binding.

Hero is a layered photo composition: three selectable real-photo scenes, a smaller detail photo, white print-like borders, subtly rotated frames and an outlined decorative COMPOSITE word. Background reuses the existing AI fiberglass artwork plus authored geometric curves suggesting material strands. Gallery photos carry similar framing. No synthetic factory or product photograph was added.

Focal motion: the photo frames arrive in a bounded 0.9-second composition sequence; the hero cycles through real photos every six seconds. Controls allow direct selection and pause. Autoplay stops on focus/hover, offscreen, hidden tabs and reduced-motion preference. Scroll motion opens selected photographic masks and the size grouping once, with content visible by default. No new animation dependency was added.
